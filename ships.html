SHIPPING TERMS

RAREPAIR

A pairing that is underexplored or has very little fan art and fan-created stories.

CRACKSHIP

An unexpected pairing, often brought about by the charm of an unlikely combination.

OTP

An abbreviation for “one true pairing,” used to refer to a favorite ship.

AU

Alternate Universe: an alternative version of the universe or of the characters' history.

Ship for fun, tag your work clearly, and remember that fans can enjoy different pairings.
