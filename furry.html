FURRY FIELD GUIDE

SUBGROUPS

Yes, there are several subgroups within the furry fandom, divided primarily by character species, visual style, or the way an individual participates in the community. Many people still hold prejudices or find it strange due to a lack of information, but being a furry is actually very simple and normal!

STYLE AND FOCUS OF INTEREST

Fursuiters: People who wear costumes of their characters. Kemono: A Japanese furry art style that is cute and features anime-like traits. Artist / Maker: Illustrators, writers, and creators of fursuits. Popufurs: The most famous artists and members within the fandom.

FURSONA SPECIES

Canines and Felines: Wolves, foxes, dogs, and cats. Scalies: Reptiles, dragons, and amphibians. Avians: Birds and griffins. Protogens and Synths: Cyborg animals with digital visors. Open Species: Original creatures from the fandom.

WE ARE NOT THERIANS, WE ARE FURRIES!

In short: Being a furry is about art, characters, and fandom. Being a therian is about identity and personal belief. Here on the site, the focus is 100% on creativity, art, and geek culture! No mystery, no weirdness: just a creative bunch sharing art and good humor!
